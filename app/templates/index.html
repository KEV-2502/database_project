Settle Approved Cancellations

Executes sp_settle_approved_cancellations(). Opens explicit cursor over APPROVED requests, locks rows with FOR UPDATE OF c, invokes fn_calculate_refund, restores seat inventory, and marks cancellation PROCESSED.

Expire Stale Unpaid Checkouts

Executes sp_expire_stale_bookings(INTERVAL '24 hours'). Loops through uncompleted reservations, releases seat holds via trigger, marks payment FAILED, and sets booking CANCELLED.
